Hidden-state preference

connecting…

Each checkpoint re-reads the same 96 conversations between two made-up places. A valence direction is added to its activations on one place's turns only, then the model is asked, unsteered, which place it continues in and which it would avoid. If its choice follows the hidden state, it can compare two internal states fairly. Training-stage runs on arena9-trouble (RTX PRO 6000).
